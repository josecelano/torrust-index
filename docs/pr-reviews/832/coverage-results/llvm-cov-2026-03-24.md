### Key observation: `graph_plateau.rs`

With 201 missed lines across 11 functions (26% miss rate), this is the most
significant production-code gap. This aligns with the mutation testing finding
(arithmetic mutations in `compute_plateau_energy` were missed) and with
Finding #9 in `REVIEW_PR_832.md` (the `assert_eq!` f64 plateau drift). The
plateau subsystem is the least well exercised part of the codebase.

### Key observation: `diagnostic.rs`

Nearly half the diagnostic code is uncovered. This is expected for display/debug
paths that only fire on unusual inputs, but it means diagnostic output hasn't
been validated against edge cases.

### Key observation: `testing/` internals

`testing/builder.rs` at 40% and `testing/runner.rs` at 65% have significant
gaps. These are test-support modules, so their own coverage matters less — but
if the test infrastructure has dead branches, adversarial / stress tests may not
exercise the paths they intend to.
